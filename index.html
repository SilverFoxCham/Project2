Classic two-player

Tic Tac Toe

How to play

  1. Share one device. Player X places the first mark.
  2. Take turns on empty cells: X is a cross, O is a circle.
  3. Get three of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win the round.
  4. Scoring: each win adds 1 point. A full board with no winner counts as a draw. Scores stay until you choose Reset scores.
